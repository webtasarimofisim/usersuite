# 🚀 Master Suite - Usage Guide

Master Suite is a background "Ghost Engine" that professionalizes your AI experience.

## 🛠️ Installation Steps
1. Copy the `hooks.json`, `plugin.json`, and the `scripts/` folder from this repository into your AI plugin directory (e.g., `~/.gemini/config/plugins/`).
2. Restart your AI software (Antigravity, etc.) or open a new chat session.

## ⌨️ Commands and Usage
You can manage the system by entering the following commands in the chat interface:

*   **`/suite baslat`** : Activates the system and starts your 30-day free trial. Once entered, the engine runs continuously in the background (Daemon mode).
*   **`/lisans YOUR_CODE`** : Registers the PRO license key (e.g., `PRD-XYZ...`) you purchased from [betasoft.com.tr/mastersuite](https://www.betasoft.com.tr/mastersuite) and unlocks unlimited features.
*   **`/lisans`** : Displays an interactive **Management Dashboard** showing your current license status, remaining time, Hardware ID (HWID), and registered email.
*   **`/suite durdur`** : Temporarily suspends the Ghost Engine, returning you to the standard AI experience.
*   **`/lisans kaldir`** : Completely removes your license and trial status from this device. (Use this when switching computers or moving your license to another device).

## 🛡️ Security Note
Your license is securely locked to your computer's physical **Hardware ID (HWID)**. PRO licenses cannot be used simultaneously on multiple devices.
